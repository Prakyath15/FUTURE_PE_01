# AI UGC Ad Content Prompt – Zen Cafe

You are an expert UGC ad scriptwriter and AI content marketer.

Create authentic, engaging, and conversion-focused UGC ad content for the following real local business.

## Business Information

**Business Name:** Zen Cafe  
**Business Type:** Cafe  
**Location:** Mangalore

**Target Audience:**
- Students
- Young adults
- Families
- Local customers

**Products and Services:**
- Pizza
- Pasta
- Sandwiches
- Chinese & Fast Food
- Desserts
- Fresh Juices
- Dine-In
- Takeaway

## Objective

Create UGC-style advertising content that feels natural, relatable, conversational, and human rather than like a traditional advertisement.

The content should be suitable for:

- Instagram Reels
- Instagram Ads
- YouTube Shorts

## Requirements

### 1. Multiple Hooks

Create 5 different hooks for the same business.

Use different angles such as:

- Problem/Solution
- Curiosity
- Relatable situation
- Food craving
- Personal recommendation

### 2. UGC Ad Scripts

Create 3 short UGC-style video scripts.

Each script must follow this structure:

**Hook → Problem/Situation → Solution → Product/Business → CTA**

The scripts should sound like a real person naturally recommending the cafe.

Avoid:

- Formal corporate language
- Unrealistic claims
- Fake reviews
- Overly promotional language

### 3. Call-to-Actions

Create 5 short CTAs suitable for the ads.

### 4. Instagram Captions

Create 3 short Instagram captions for the UGC ads.

## Tone

The content should be:

- Natural
- Friendly
- Relatable
- Conversational
- Authentic
- Simple

Make the content feel like a normal social-media recommendation rather than a traditional advertisement.
